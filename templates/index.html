ABOUT OUR DEPARTMENT

Department Overview

The basis for many of the technical developments that we see in the world today is Computer Science. The field has expanded dramatically and continues to contribute to technological advancement.

Computer Science is used across numerous multidisciplinary domains and plays an important role in developing highly qualified engineers and technology professionals.

Our department is committed to quality education, student competency, innovation and technological excellence, preparing students to meet the evolving needs of industry and society.

OUR VISION

Vision

To produce globally competent Engineers in Computer Science and Engineering through outcome-based education, cutting-edge research, critical thinking, hands-on skills development, and adherence to ethical principles, while fostering responsible citizens of India who contribute to societal and global growth.

Mission
