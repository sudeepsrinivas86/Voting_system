ABOUT THE DEPARTMENT

Department Overview

The Department of Computer Science and Engineering is committed to providing quality technical education while preparing students to meet the rapidly changing demands of the computing and technology industry.

The department focuses on building strong fundamentals in programming, computer science concepts, software development and emerging technologies. Students are encouraged to develop problem-solving abilities, analytical thinking, creativity and professional skills.

Through academic learning, practical exposure, technical activities and association initiatives, the department provides students with opportunities to transform their knowledge into real-world applications.

Department Journey

The Department of Computer Science and Engineering commenced its academic journey in 2008, with an initial sanctioned intake of 60 students. For more than a decade, from 2008 to 2024, the department continued with an intake of 60 students per academic year. With the growing demand for Computer Science education and the department's continued development, the approved intake was enhanced to 120 students from 2024 onwards. This expansion reflects the department's commitment to providing greater opportunities for students to pursue quality education in the field of Computer Science and Engineering.
